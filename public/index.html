How Real Mobile WhatsApp Automation Operates

When a customer clicks your website widget or scans the QR code, their phone opens WhatsApp with the exact pre-filled message. For your number to automatically reply back inside their WhatsApp, choose one of these two proven production methods:

Method A: WhatsApp Web QR Pairing (Linked Devices)

Scan the QR code at the top of this dashboard using WhatsApp > Linked Devices > Link a Device. Once linked, the server automatically reads incoming texts and replies directly into your customer chats!

Method B: Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (Graph API v21.0)

For enterprise interactive buttons, interactive lists, and official green-tick business numbers via Meta:

  1. Connect your phone number on Meta Developer Portal.
  2. Point your Meta Webhook to your public domain (e.g. https://your-domain.com/api/webhook).
  3. Enter your Meta Access Token into .env.
